Purpose

AGOS aims to inform and educate people about the importance of water resources and the environmental issues that may affect them, especially in the Philippines. It focuses on topics such as El Niño, water conservation, and the possible effects of technological developments such as Pax Silica, including AI and semiconductor industries, on our environment.

The website was created to help people understand how these issues may affect our daily lives, communities, and most importantly our natural resources. It also serves as a source of information for students, teachers, and the general public who want to learn more about water security and environmental sustainability.

Through AGOS, users can explore such as educational articles, environmental data, interactive maps, and quizzes that make their learning more easier and more exciting. It also provides simple tips on how to save water and avoid wasting an important resource. Overall, our goal is to encourage everyone to become more aware of our environmental problems and understand that protecting our water resources is a shared responsibility. We believe that even a small actions, but when practiced by many people, it can help make a difference in creating a better and more sustainable future.
